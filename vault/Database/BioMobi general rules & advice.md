
First, this project will set the basis for the development of a BioMobi dataset. As a result, BIOLOOP data on biomass properties, application-dependent quality characteristics, quantity, geolocation and seasonal variability will be collected from existing datasets at UGent and publicly available datasets and stored.

The most relevant parameters will be mapped including gross composition, volumes, seasonality, physical characteristics of the side streams, as well as locations of production/availability of the streams. When unreliable or incomplete, information gaps will be filled with data available in (scientific) literature.
